# Product

**What it is.** Headway is an explorer for one year of MBTA subway service, built on a data pipeline the author owns end to end.

**Who it is for.** Hiring managers and analysts judging the work, and curious riders. They arrive cold, so the first screen must make sense without explanation and reward exploring.

**Its job.** Let someone find when and where service broke down, see why (alerts, weather, planned work), and know how far to trust each number.

**Mode.** Operate: an app for a task, where precision and honesty outrank decoration. The year chart is the one memorable moment.

**Voice.** Plain, sentence case, active. No hype. Say what the data cannot show as clearly as what it can.

**Principles.** Never show a missing day as zero. Show coverage next to every claim. Prefer the measure that survives disruptions (long gaps) over the one that does not (late arrivals).
